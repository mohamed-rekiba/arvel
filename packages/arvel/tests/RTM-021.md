| FR-021-17 | `discover_commands` tolerates any Exception | `test_wi_021_console_hardening.py::TestDiscoverCommandsWidening::*` (2 tests) | **GREEN** |

> **FR-021-15 / FR-021-16 — withdrawn.** The original rationale assumed `except A, B:` was a Python 2 leftover. It is not: [PEP 758](https://peps.python.org/pep-0758/) (Python 3.14) makes the unparenthesized multi-class form first-class syntax equivalent to `except (A, B):`. `ruff format` 0.15+ canonicalizes to the unparenthesized form, so enforcing parens would fight the formatter on every commit. We accept the formatter's choice; the architecture test and the pre-commit hook were removed in Stage 4 (QA-Post).
